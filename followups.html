How the monitor works

Why it's empty — activate the feed

A 12-month scan of fparro@gmail.com found no inbound requests from @flowauto.com colleagues. That's expected: your work requests go to FPARRO@flowauto.com, which you read in Apple Mail. Apple Mail is a viewer running on your Mac — the automation runs in the cloud and can't see it. So the mail has to be reached at the server, not on your Mac.

Forwarding is the only route. Connecting the work account directly was ruled out: Claude holds a single Gmail connection and it's bound to fparro@gmail.com — there's no second slot, and freeing it would break the Gmail cleanup, Freshdesk check-request, and morning-brief routines that run on the personal account.

  • Forward work mail to this Gmail. Sign in to your work mail on the web — not Apple Mail — then Settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP → "Add a forwarding address" → fparro@gmail.com. (Outlook/Exchange instead: Settings → Mail → Forwarding.)
  • Confirm the address. Google emails a confirmation code to fparro@gmail.com. That's a mailbox the automation can read, so the code can be retrieved and handed back to you.
  • Forward everything addressed to you. Back in work-mail Settings → Filters → new filter on to:fparro@flowauto.com → "Forward it to" your gmail. Server-side, so it runs whether or not your Mac is on.
  • Turn on Google Chat email notifications. Chat/Gmail → Settings → Chat and Meet → notifications → email for direct messages & @-mentions. This is the only way Chat asks reach the tracker.

Don't build the rule inside Apple Mail — those only fire while Mail.app is open on your Mac. It has to be server-side.

Once mail starts flowing in, the next 8 AM / 3 PM sweep populates this page and starts the digests automatically — no further action needed.
